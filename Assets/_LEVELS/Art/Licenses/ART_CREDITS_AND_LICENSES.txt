Technical changes
  The graphics were cropped and combined into SamuraiGreen.png and NinjaAdventureWorld.png,
  sliced with aligned sprite pivots, and imported with point filtering at 16 pixels per unit.
  Some scenery instances are flipped or tinted in Unity. The well uses pixels from the original water tile,
  tinted and composited into the opening of the original rim.
  The original Godot example tree has no separate license file. The license notice is on the pack page.

Technical changes
  The five sheets were sliced into 16 × 32 or 32 × 32 frames and combined into Goblin.png.
  Feet pivots were aligned and the presentation is scaled in Unity. No source-pixel palette replacement.
  The original ZIP contains only the five graphics. The license notice is on the download page.
